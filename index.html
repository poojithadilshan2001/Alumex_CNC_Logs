OPERATIONAL STANDARDS

OEE Calculation Framework

Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) identifies the percentage of manufacturing time that is truly productive. An OEE score of 100% means you are manufacturing only good parts, as fast as possible, with no stop time.

World-Class: 85%+
Typical: ~60%
Low: < 40%
The Ultimate Metric

OEE = A × P × Q

TPM standard. Takes into account all losses to show true plant productivity.

Availability (A)

Op Min / Planned Min

Measures Downtime Loss. Breakdowns & tooling changes relative to 12h shifts.

Performance (P)

(Tot Pcs × Cycle) / Op Min

Measures Speed Loss. Minor stops and reduced speed vs theoretical targets.

Quality (Q)

Good Pcs / Total Pcs

Measures Quality Loss. Accounts for rejected profiles (scrap) and rework.
