CNC 5000 OPERATIONAL STANDARDS

OEE Calculation Framework & Shifts

12-Hour Operational Shift Base
OEE Formula

OEE = A × P × Q

Composite score evaluating uptime availability, speed performance, and defect-free quality.

Availability (A)

Operating / Planned Min

Planned = 720m (12h) - 90m breaks = 630m. Operating = Planned - Total Downtime.

Performance (P)

Ideal Time / Operating Min

Ideal time = Σ (Parts Produced × Profile Cycle Time) / 60.

Quality (Q)

Good Parts / Total Parts

Good parts = Total parts produced minus scrap and dimensional rejects.
